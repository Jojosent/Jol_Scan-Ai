Leaflet, Chart.js, fonts and map tiles are fetched by the browser from external
CDNs/services. Their copyright/license notices stay with those distributions.
Map attribution is kept visible in every map. Standard OpenStreetMap raster tiles
are darkened locally using CSS; no map API key is needed. Tile usage follows
https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/tiles/ (no bulk download or prefetch).
Public CDN/tile availability and
service limits are independent of this project.

No YOLO weights, Hugging Face models, pothole training datasets, third-party
road photos or paid stock media are included or required. The original request's
keremberke model is intentionally replaced by the user's Gemini-only provider.
The Gemini training dataset is not distributed here; its complete composition
and licenses are not disclosed by this project and are not represented as open.
No local model training is performed.

All demo images are procedural drawings generated by scripts/seed_demo.py.
The SVG hero illustration and favicon are original code-native artwork.
